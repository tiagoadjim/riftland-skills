# Scene patterns

For each beat of the script, pick a visual that shows the idea instead of illustrating the words. Each pattern lists what it is good for and what to keep simple. Scenes are built with `motion_sandbox_write` from the brand kit; `motion_catalog` has moves to borrow.

## Hook
- Big question: the question in large type, one object reacting to it. Keep it under 3 seconds.
- Surprising number: the number counting up from zero to the claim.

## Definition
- Label and arrow: the object in the center, the term appearing beside it, one line of meaning.
- Before and after word: the everyday word crossing out into the precise one.

## Process
- Numbered steps: step circles appearing in order with an icon each, a line connecting them.
- Conveyor: items moving left to right through a stage that changes them.

## Comparison
- Two columns: the same rows for both sides, the winner highlighted at the end.
- Slider: a split frame wiping from one state to the other.

## Numbers and growth
- Chart build: bars or a line drawing in as the voice says each value; show the exact figures.
- Stacked blocks: a total made of colored pieces, each labeled as it lands.
- Timeline: a line with dates or years popping on it.

## List
- Cards flying in: one card per item with an icon and two words, three or four at most.
- Check marks: items ticking off in sequence.

## Metaphor
- A familiar object stands for the idea (a snowball for compound growth, a funnel for filtering). One metaphor per video.

## Recap and call to action
- Recap: the beat labels appearing as a row of icons.
- Call to action: the logo and one line, held for the last two seconds, brand colors.

## Rules
- One idea per scene and one move per idea.
- Keep on-screen words to what the viewer can read in the scene's time.
- Captions are separate from the scene text: follow the voice, in the brand's body font.
- Keep contrast high and margins safe for the vertical formats (the top and bottom 10 percent can be covered by the app's own interface).
- Check frames with `motion_sandbox_check` at each scene before rendering.
