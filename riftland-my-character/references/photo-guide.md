# Photos that make a good character

One excellent main photo matters more than ten average ones: the build and the polished versions work from the main image.

## The main photo
- One person only, the face fully visible, looking at the camera or a slight turn.
- Sharp and well lit, with soft light from the front or a window. No hard shadows across the face.
- Relaxed expression, mouth closed or a small smile.
- Head and shoulders, or down to the waist. The face fills a good part of the frame.
- At least 1000 pixels on the short side, not a screenshot of a screenshot.
- Natural skin, no heavy filter, no beauty mode.

## Supporting photos (up to 8 as `sheet_images`, 2 to 4 is plenty)
- A side or three-quarter view.
- A full-body view standing, in simple clothes.
- A different expression, such as a real smile.
- The same period of life: photos years apart confuse the identity.

## What to avoid
- Sunglasses, hats low on the forehead, masks, hands over the face.
- Group photos, mirrors, other faces in the frame.
- Strong filters, heavy retouching, cartoon effects.
- Extreme angles, wide-angle distortion, very dark or backlit photos.
- Photos of other people, or of people who have not agreed.

## How to ask
- "Mandame una foto de frente, con buena luz y sin lentes de sol." Then, if possible, one de perfil y una de cuerpo entero.
- When the user has only selfies, take the sharpest one as the main photo and say why.
- Photos in a chat: `media_upload` for each, or `media_upload_widget` when there is no shell.

## Checking the character
Look at the saved character and a first image with `media_view` and compare:
1. The face shape and the eyes.
2. The hair line and color.
3. Distinctive marks (freckles, a scar) that should stay.
4. The age: not younger or older than the person.
If two of these differ, ask for a better main photo before building anything.

## Words for the description
One line on what must not change: age range, hair, marks, signature style. Not a story and not a personality.
