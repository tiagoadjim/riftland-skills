# Looks by intent

Art direction for the `prompt` of a Marketing Studio call, or for the words of a `create` request. Use the user's own words first; add from this list only what they left open, one or two items per group. The server writes the final prompt: you are giving direction, not syntax.

## Catalog (clean studio)
- Surface: seamless paper sweep, matte stone slab, brushed metal, glass with a soft reflection.
- Light: one large soft source from above, a bounce card on the shadow side, a thin gradient strip along one edge for a glossy product.
- Frame: product centered, nearly filling the frame, a gentle contact shadow, nothing else.
- Leave out: props, text, patterns, a busy background. A catalog image is the product and nothing else.
- Ask yourself: could a store page use this unchanged?

## In use
- Moment: the second right after the product is used, not a pose: pouring, unpacking, closing the lid, setting it on a table.
- Setting: a believable place where the product lives (kitchen counter, trail, desk), with two or three honest props.
- Light: daylight from a window or open shade; warm lamp light for evening products.
- Leave out: staged smiles, brand logos of other products, clutter that competes with the label.

## Hands
- Whose hands: say age range and nails only when it matters (a beauty product); otherwise leave it open.
- Action: holding it toward the lens, applying, opening, comparing sizes against the palm.
- Frame: cropped at the wrist or the torso, the product at the sharpest point.

## On a model
- Close-up: the product beside the face, soft beauty light, shallow depth.
- Full body: clothing or footwear shown whole, a plain backdrop, one clear pose.
- Editorial: a styled set, dramatic light, the product as the star of the outfit.
- A saved character keeps the same person across looks: `avatar_id` in Marketing Studio, `character_id` in `create`.

## Vertical pin (2:3)
- Keep a calm area in the top third (a headline will go there) and the product in the lower two thirds.
- A scene with depth reads better as a pin than a plain sweep: surface, one prop, side light.

## Wide banner (16:9 or 21:9)
- Product on one third, empty space on the other two for text, in a scene that keeps a horizon line.
- Say which side the space goes on if the page layout needs it.

## Ad stills (a pack with `create`)
- One idea, three or four angles on it: wide, medium, detail, in-hand.
- The same surface family and the same palette in every image; light and angle change, not the world.

## Conceptual
- One impossible thing only (it floats, it splashes, it sits on a cloud). Keep the label sharp and the product shape true.
- Name the material of the surprise (water, powder, paper, light) so the look is specific.

## New version (`change_of`)
- The request is the change alone: "cambiá el fondo a una mesa de madera", "más luz cálida". Nothing about the rest.
- A new background, a new light or a new color each is one change; two changes are two requests.

## When results come out generic
- Name a specific surface and a specific light instead of an adjective ("marble counter, window light from the left" beats "premium").
- Reduce the props to one.
- Check the photo first (see the photo guide): most generic results start with a weak reference.
